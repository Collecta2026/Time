Time — user guide

HR & payroll for companies under Egyptian labour law. Switch language any time with the toggle at the top right; Arabic flips the whole screen right-to-left.

Roles & who does what

Access follows a scheme of delegation. HR prepares payroll and manages people, attendance, leave and loans. The Finance Manager approves payroll, sees all finance information, prints the schedules and marks runs paid. The Managing Director gives the joint authorisation (final release). The Administrator manages users and settings. A Viewer can look but not change. The person who prepared a payroll run cannot approve it.

Setting up (Administrator)

Open Settings and enter the company details, the paying bank, and confirm the statutory parameters — social insurance rates, the insurable-wage floor and ceiling, the income-tax exemption, working hours, overtime premiums and the leave policy. These can be changed whenever the law changes; confirm the insurable-wage ceiling every January (it rises 15%). Set the scheme of delegation bands here too: for each band of net payroll, tick whether Finance Manager approval alone is enough or a joint MD release is also required. Then create your people under Users and give each the right role.

Building the organisation

Departments and Grades & Spine come first. A grade holds spine points, each carrying a monthly salary, so an employee sits on a point with visible room to grow to the points above. Then add Employees: personal details, department, grade and spine point, start date, contract type, salary and allowances, bank details and medical plan. The employee code should match the worker's fingerprint ID so attendance imports line up.

Attendance

Attendance is imported from your fingerprint system. On the Attendance page, download the CSV template, fill it (employee code, date, and either hours worked or in/out times) and upload it. Overtime and shortfall are worked out per day against the standard hours in Settings.

Leave, loans and benefits

Record Leave by type (annual, sick, casual, maternity, unpaid) with a paid percentage. Issue Loans with a monthly deduction; the balance reduces automatically when payroll is authorised. Set up Medical benefits plans with employer and employee monthly shares and enrol employees on their record.

Running payroll — the approval flow

On Payroll, HR picks the period and runs it; the system computes gross, overtime, social insurance, income tax, loan and absence deductions, and net pay for every active employee. HR then submits for approval. The Finance Manager approves; if the delegation band requires it, the Managing Director authorises for payment (loan balances reduce at this point). The Finance Manager then marks it paid. Any approver can return a run to HR with a reason. Every step is stamped with the name and time and written to the Audit log.

What Finance receives

From an open run, print or export three documents. The Payment schedule lists each employee's net pay with bank and account, the total to disburse, and the statutory remittances. The Insurance remittance schedule is the auditable social-insurance return for the authority (NOSI) — per employee insurable wage and the 11% / 18.75% / 1% split, with the approval trail printed on it. Individual payslips print from each employee's record or the run.

Reports

Under Reports: attendance, sick days, starters & leavers, management statistics, the insurance remittance register (every month's return for the year, each linking to its auditable schedule), the leave-pay provision, and the loan register. Each exports to Excel.

Leave-pay provision (for Finance)

The Leave pay provision report values the accrued-but-untaken statutory annual leave each employee has earned — the liability Finance carries. Choose the year and month to see accrued days, days taken, the balance, and its money value per employee and in total, plus the month's accrual charge. The Finance Manager can post the provision for a month to save a dated snapshot; the movement table then shows the liability month by month for audit.

Contracts

Each employee record prints an Arabic employment contract, populated from their details, as required by Law 14/2025.

Figures are implemented as published, but tax and insurance rates move — keep Settings current and have payroll output checked before first live use. The full installation and administration guide is supplied as a separate document with the software.
